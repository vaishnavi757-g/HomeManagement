🏠 About Us

Welcome to the Home Management System!

Home Management System is a simple and user-friendly web application designed to help users manage their daily household activities in one place.

✨ Our Features

  • 📋 Manage daily tasks
  • 💰 Track household expenses
  • 🛒 Manage shopping items
  • 🔐 User Login and Registration
  • 📊 User-specific dashboard

🎯 Our Goal

Our goal is to provide a simple and convenient platform for organizing household activities, tracking expenses, and managing shopping items efficiently.

👩‍💻 About the Developer

This Home Management System was developed as a web application project using Python Flask and SQLite.
